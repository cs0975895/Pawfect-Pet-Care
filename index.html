Our Conservation Efforts

Education is lifesaving. By educating the public about mustelid lives and habitats, we can not only make ferret owners happy, but our environment as a whole, outside of the mustelids that are kept as pets. Otters, stoats, badgers, and other mustelids are key parts of our ecosystem's health, and a further understanding for the public makes all their lives better!

Rehabilitation gives all a chance. While nature doesn't create all mustelids equally, we believe all deserve the chance at life. If an injured, sick, neglected, or abused mustelid is brought into our care, we do absolutely everything in our power to bring it back to 100%, and facilitate a natural return to form for the creature.

Without volunteers, none of this is possible. Without human help, none of our efforts are possible, so we encourage volunteering for all capable of doing so. Whether that be through donations to our facility, or state and federal resources, the human element is the lifeblood of care for mustelids, and all other creatures. Inquire with a staff member or conservation officer on how you can help your local mustelid population!
